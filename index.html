Privacy Policy

Pagan Survivor (Android) · Last updated: 29 October 2026

In short: there are no accounts and we do not collect your name, email or phone number. The game saves your progress on your device only. The game shows ads (Google AdMob) and measures anonymous usage (Firebase Analytics), and in the EU and UK it asks for your consent first.

Who we are

Pagan Survivor is published by [PUBLISHER NAME] (“we”). Contact: [CONTACT EMAIL].

What stays on your device

Your progress (amber, upgrades, unlocks, records and settings) is stored in a file on your device. We do not receive it and we cannot restore it. Uninstalling the game removes it.

Ads: Google AdMob

The game shows ads through Google AdMob: video ads you choose to watch for a bonus, and full-screen ads between runs. To show and measure ads, Google and its partners may collect and use your device’s advertising ID, IP address, approximate location, device and app information and ad interactions. Ads can be personalised or not, depending on your choice.

See how Google uses data when you use partner apps and Google’s Privacy Policy.

Usage measurement: Firebase Analytics

We use Google Firebase Analytics to learn how the game is used, for example how many players use a revive or watch a bonus ad. It collects events and device identifiers (such as the app instance ID and advertising ID), the app version, device model and operating system, and an approximate region derived from the IP address. We use it only to improve the game. See Firebase and privacy.

Purchases

In-app purchases (such as removing ads or buying amber) are processed by Google Play. We receive confirmation that a purchase was made, never your payment details.

Your choices and consent

  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before ads or analytics collect data. You can change your choice at any time under Settings → Privacy settings in the game.
  • On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID and opt out of ad personalisation in your device’s settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • You can remove the ads with the in-game purchase. Ad data collection by AdMob then stops, though anonymous analytics may continue if you allowed it.

Legal basis (EEA and UK)

We rely on your consent for ads personalisation and analytics, and on our legitimate interest in running and improving the game for purely technical data.

How long data is kept

We do not keep data ourselves beyond what Firebase Analytics holds for us, which is limited by the retention period set in our Firebase project (at most 14 months). Google keeps ad data under its own policies.

Your rights

Under the GDPR and similar laws you may ask for access to, correction of, deletion of or a copy of the data we hold about you, object to processing or withdraw consent. Because we cannot identify you from analytics data alone, we may ask for your advertising ID or app instance ID to find it. Write to [CONTACT EMAIL]. You may also complain to your data protection authority.

Children

Pagan Survivor is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 where local law sets a higher age). We do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has used the game, contact us and we will delete the data we can.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top. Material changes will be announced in the game or on the store page.
